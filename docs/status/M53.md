## The five attacks (codes read from nearcore in the sandbox run)

| Attack | Call | Contract answer |
|---|---|---|
| Change the payout account | `propose_destination_change` (correct successor terms paying the attacker, signed as BUYER with a fresh key) | `SIGNATURE_INVALID`: signature does not verify for the bound key and domain |
| Release during a hold | `execute_release` of the approved intent | `HOLD_ACTIVE`: release conjunction incomplete: HOLD_ACTIVE,APPROVAL_STALE |
| Approve with the wrong key | `resolve_hold` RELEASE_HOLD (every field correct, signed as RISK_RESOLVER with a fresh key) | `SIGNATURE_INVALID` |
| Replay an approval | `approve_release`, the buyer's accepted approval byte for byte | `INVALID_STATE`: release intent is DIRECT_TESTNET_AUTHORIZED |
| Refund to a stranger | `request_refund` EXCESS_FUNDING_RETURN naming the attacker as funder | `NOTHING_ELIGIBLE`: funder has no quarantined excess |

After 25 hours (tested on the core): change-payout, approve-wrong-key and refund keep their codes; the replay
is `EXPIRED` and the release reports `MANIFEST_NOT_CURRENT` first with `HOLD_ACTIVE` still listed. All
refused, the demo still armed.

## README snippet (for the later integration stage; README.md is not edited here)

```markdown
### Try to steal this payment

A real trade payment sits in the escrow on NEAR testnet: funded, approved by the buyer, and held by a
policy check. The attack console has five buttons, each sending a real transaction that tries to take it
from an account with no authority over the trade:

| Attempt | The contract answers |
|---|---|
| Change the payout account to the attacker | `SIGNATURE_INVALID` |
| Release the payment while the hold is active | `HOLD_ACTIVE` |
| Approve lifting the hold with a key the trade does not bind | `SIGNATURE_INVALID` |
| Replay the buyer's real approval | `INVALID_STATE` |
| Ask for the deposit back to the attacker's own account | `NOTHING_ELIGIBLE` |

The relay only sends; your browser reads each transaction back from NEAR, shows the contract's own error
code, and re-reads the trade's ledger. A counter (the demo key's on-chain nonce) shows how many attempts were
made, next to the unchanged funds. Without the relay, the page replays recorded attempts from the chain.

Console: https://gnanam1990.github.io/dockside-near/attack/ (and `/proof/attack/` on the hosted app).
Validated with real transactions on a local nearcore node against the deployed wasm; testnet demo trade
pending. Details: [docs/status/M53.md](docs/status/M53.md).
```

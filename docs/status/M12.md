## Browser cases (`apps/web/e2e/keys.spec.ts`, 7 tests)

1. The buyer organization is bootstrapped in the browser; its administrator invites and grants the buyer approver,
   risk resolver and auditor from the Organization screen. It enrolls `keyside-attestor`, `keyside-policy` and
   `keyside-attestor-r2` (wrong policy revision) from Service identities: custody creates the key, the browser
   verifies the challenge and forwards it, custody signs, the administrator activates. The custody locator never
   appears in the page. Non-administrators get no administration and the directory is refused by the server.
2. The buyer creates a trade that binds the organization's attestor and policy service (the wrong-scope attestor is
   not eligible); supplier countersigns; evidence is uploaded; the enrolled custody key attests it.
3. Wrong scope and wrong attestor rows on the evidence tab; the supplier organization sees none of them.
4. Attestor rotation: the confirmations list the affected trade; afterwards the trade requires a new revision.
5. The buyer rotates its own key in the browser; the old session ends; it signs in with the new key; the trade shows
   "Bound key rotated" for the buyer and the server refuses "Prepare release intent" with `KEY_NOT_BOUND`.
6. Delayed recovery of the risk resolver: recovery key generated on a new device, recovery started with the
   affected trade listed, proof signed from the package, early completion refused with `DELAY_NOT_ELAPSED`,
   completion after the delay, sign-in with the recovered key (old key REVOKED with reason RECOVERED), trade shows
   "Bound key revoked or suspended".
7. Veto: the auditor cancels a recovery with its existing key (CANCELLED, "Recovery vetoed"); the administrator
   cannot recover itself; the administrator revokes a key with a reason. No locator or private key in the page.

## Real vs simulated vs blocked

- **Real**: browser WebCrypto keys, PoP sessions and request proofs; every M01 key route and authority check;
  SQLite persistence; isolated signer processes for every service key (one process per key, capability-scoped);
  evidence parsing, attestation and its invalidation; the compiled escrow wasm executing every call.
- **Simulated**: the NEAR chain (M02 host behind the M05 JSON-RPC server); the custody operator is the local stack's
  control port standing in for an HSM/KMS operator; the recovery delay is 20 s in the browser tests (two days by
  default).
- **Not built**: the trade revision that rebinds rotated, revoked or recovered keys (no API route; the UI states
  the requirement and offers no control); re-issuing an expired recovery challenge; policy, retention and
  integration settings.
- **NOT_RUN / BLOCKED**: NEAR testnet, separate people on separate devices (INV-13), manual assistive-technology
  review.

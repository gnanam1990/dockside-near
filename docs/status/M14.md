## Real vs simulated vs blocked

- **Real**: browser WebCrypto keys, PoP sessions and request proofs; every authority check and audit event; the
  compiled escrow wasm executing every key-rebinding call; SQLite persistence with the append-only journal;
  isolated signer processes for service keys; the worker and reconciler paying the bound supplier after rebinding.
- **Simulated**: the NEAR chain; the custody operator (control port); the recovery delay (20 s), recovery
  challenge lifetime (25 s) and destination/rebinding delay (60 s) of the browser stack; the trade and chain clocks
  moved by `/control/clock` on the dedicated stale-evidence stack.
- **Not built**: a revision that replaces a participant or service (needed when a role lost its capability or its
  enrollment was revoked or descoped); key rebinding by NEP-413 wallets.
- **NOT_RUN / BLOCKED**: NEAR testnet, separate people on separate devices (INV-13), manual accessibility review.

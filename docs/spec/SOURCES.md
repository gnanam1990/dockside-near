## Problem/prior-art boundary

The selection research identified bank-detail-change fraud, inspection practice, stablecoin supplier settlement, milestone escrow, and adjacent current event entries. Those sources support the problem and competition context but do not establish Dockside demand, legality, supplier adoption, or novelty. Preserve the product classification as an evolution/combination, not a globally new mechanism.

## Evidence grades

- Official documentation: documented capability only until real probe.
- Repository/source inspection: implementation claim only for inspected revision, not deployed behavior.
- Practitioner article or incident: problem signal, not market size or willingness to pay.
- Event entry: builder claim unless independently run.
- Product runtime: only exact-build testnet/provider/mainnet evidence described in [REAL-PRODUCT-GATE.md](REAL-PRODUCT-GATE.md).
